# Safari Park

Safari Park is a 3D simulation and management game developed in Unity 6. Players are responsible for building and running a safari park filled with wildlife, visitors, and interconnected systems.

## Gameplay Overview

In this simulation-driven experience, players oversee the park’s operations and ecosystem:

- Buy and sell animals, buildings, trees, feeders, water dispensers, and vehicles.
- Hire rangers who can hunt animals when instructed.
- Build roads to define tour paths for vehicles.
- Tour vehicles automatically find a path from the entrance to the exit, take visitors on a tour, and return to the entrance.
- Generate income through visitor tickets.
- Upgrade the parking garage to accommodate more vehicles.
- Purchase and place various types of trees to decorate the park and support the ecosystem.
- Feeders are available for both carnivores and herbivores.
- Water dispensers serve both types of animals.
- Animals behave autonomously: seeking food and water, forming groups, breeding, and hunting.
- Players progress through a leveling system with 15 levels.
  - Unlock new items, features, and achievements by reaching higher levels.
- The game features 15 achievements that track major milestones.
- The game ends when:
  - All achievements are completed
  - The player runs out of money
  - Visitor happiness drops below a critical threshold

## Key Features

- Full safari park construction and management
- Road-building system for defining tour paths
- Economy system based on visitor tickets and asset trading
- Autonomous animal simulation: hunger, thirst, breeding, grouping, and predation
- Hireable ranger units for wildlife control
- Upgradable vehicle garage
- Grid-based building and placement system
- Leveling system with 15 progression levels
- 15 unlockable achievements
- Ecosystem balancing influenced by player decisions

## Development Details

- Built with **Unity 6** (6000.0.37f1)
- Structured using the **MVCS (Model-View-Controller-Service)** architecture
- Uses **custom shaders** for trees, grass, and hover effects
- Designed for multiple input types (mouse, gamepad)
- Developed with PC platforms in mind
